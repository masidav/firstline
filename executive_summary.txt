Executive Summary: Fan-First Guaranteed Ticketing Platform
1. Business Overview
    • Company Name Idea: TrueFan Access / VerifyTicket
      
    • Core Value Proposition: A B2B2C SaaS and infrastructure layer partnering directly with concert promoters and artists to allocate a dedicated percentage of event tickets exclusively to verified top fans—guaranteeing access at face value, eliminating scalper markup, and ensuring artists retain control over their audience relationships.
      
    • The Problem: Scalpers and bots use automated scripts to capture prime inventory during public on-sales, reselling tickets at exorbitant markups on secondary markets. Neither the artist nor the promoter sees any of this secondary revenue, and loyal fans are priced out or forced to buy from third-party resellers.
      
    • The Solution: An identity-verification and allocation layer that integrates into the promoter's inventory workflow, allowing fans to prove their loyalty (via streaming history, direct-to-consumer store purchases, or verified attendance history) and unlock secure, non-transferable access to face-value tickets before the general public sale.
      
2. Market Opportunity & Target Audience
    • Target Promoters: Independent and major promoters (Live Nation, AEG, independent regional promoters) looking to reward loyal fanbases, combat bad PR from scalping scandals, and capture direct first-party data.
      
    • Target Artists: Mid-tier to stadium-level touring artists who want to protect their fan communities from exploitative pricing.
      
    • Target End-Users (Fans): Dedicated music enthusiasts who actively stream, buy merchandise, and attend live shows, but are routinely beaten by automated bots on primary ticketing sites.
      
3. Product & Technology Architecture
    • Fan Verification Engine: An OAuth and metadata integration layer connecting with major streaming platforms (Spotify API, Apple Music MusicKit) and direct store-purchasing platforms to calculate true fan affinity.
      
    • Primary Ticketing Integration: Operates as an authorization and whitelisting layer that communicates directly with primary ticketing APIs (Ticketmaster, AXS, SeatGeek) to reserve specific seats within the artist/promoter allocation pool.
      
    • Anti-Scalping Safeguards: Utilizes non-transferable reservation tokens, delayed delivery mechanisms, or face-value ticket-exchange protocols to prevent the immediate flipping of reserved inventory.
      
4. Revenue Model
    • Promoter / Ticketing SaaS Fee: A flat software licensing fee per event or a small percentage take-rate on tickets processed through the verification infrastructure.
      
    • Fan-Side Convenience/Service Subscriptions: A minor, transparent curation fee paid by the fan in exchange for guaranteed access and peace of mind (avoiding dynamic pricing and secondary market gouging).
      
5. Go-To-Market Strategy
    1. Pilot Phase: Partner with independent mid-tier touring acts (artists drawing 2,000 to 10,000-capacity venues) and progressive independent promoters who have direct control over venue allocations.
       
    2. Case Study & Social Proof: Demonstrate higher sell-through rates, zero empty seats, zero scalping complaints, and direct first-party data acquisition for the artist.
       
    3. Scaling: Expand integrations into major primary ticketer ecosystems and pitch directly to major booking agencies and management firms.
